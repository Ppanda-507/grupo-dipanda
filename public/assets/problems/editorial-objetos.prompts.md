## informacao-dispersa

Ficheiro: informacao-dispersa-editorial-v1.png

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: ONE final landscape 4:3 image for a Dipanda business intelligence consultancy problem card. Four user images are provided as VISUAL INSPIRATION ONLY: reference 1 uses sharp colored central subject against monochrome repetition; reference 2 uses restrained clean white studio photography and a strong burden metaphor; reference 3 uses editorial collage, bold hierarchy and coral gestural accents; reference 4 uses conceptual studio photography, white background and blue minimal graphics. Borrow these composition principles, NEVER their people or exact slogans. The user explicitly wants NO PEOPLE. No humans, hands, faces, body parts, silhouettes, illustrated characters or portraits even in screens. Create an elegant, serious, conceptual EDITORIAL PHOTOGRAPHIC COLLAGE using ONLY real business OBJECTS, cropped photographic cut-outs with physically convincing materials and subtle grounding shadows on an off-white/pale-gray studio background. A few muted cobalt/navy geometric accents and one lime #D6FD70 or coral #FF6A3D accent. Crisp image, no noisy grain, no 3D cartoon render, no cartoon icons, no holograms, no generic busy office photo. A strong centered photographic object arrangement must express the problem at 450px display size, safe center 80% for responsive crops. Make all four images a coherent series with same soft side light, gray paper and navy office equipment. No logos, watermarks, English ad slogans, or large headline typography: the website already supplies headings and body text. Only small functional labels or financial numbers explicitly requested in the specific scene may be included. No people is a strict invariant. Scene: fragmenting business information. Use a carefully art-directed collage of an open slim silver laptop with spreadsheet, a tablet with a small different bar chart, navy binder, separate invoice sheets, disconnected sticky notes and loose report pages. Arrange these as visibly separate islands around the center, slightly staggered or rotated with gaps, photographed from an elevated near frontal angle against clean white. Central laptop remains in restrained cobalt color while peripheral repeated reports are desaturated grayscale, inspired by reference 1's central color contrast. Two thin coral broken/dashed graphic strokes between isolated paper groups suggest sources that fail to join, understated and geometric, no flowchart icon soup. Paper content is small realistic tables and graph shapes, no headline. Information exists everywhere, but not in one unified place.
