## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: final landscape background illustration for Dipanda's responsive website hero.
Input image 1: reference image for the snowy blue mountains, penguin seen from behind, cinematic scale and photographic texture. This is a composition/style reference.
Primary request: reinterpret this scene for a Business Intelligence consultancy hero whose real HTML headline is “Transforme dados em Decisões estratégicas” and whose real HTML primary button is “CONSULTORIA GRATUITA”. Replace the Adobe icons and all other writing with empty blue sky: the penguin must now look upward toward the real headline and call to action which the website will overlay in the upper central area.
Composition/framing: WIDE LANDSCAPE panoramic image, ideally 2048 x 1152. Deep blue open sky occupies the upper 55% with clear negative space in the top-center for white text and buttons. Snowy mountains and a rising snow path occupy the lower 45%. A small realistic penguin, rear view, in the lower LEFT third, walking up the snow path, head and body aimed toward the upper CENTER. Penguin about 14% of image height, feet entirely visible and above the bottom 12% safe zone. The path leads the eye upward and toward the center call to action area, not toward any product icons. The lower center must have clean snow with space for a narrow tools strip overlaid by the website.
Style: cinematic photorealistic compositing with detailed snow, natural small tracks, atmospheric distant peaks, soft light, premium uncluttered composition. Match the reference's blue mountain mood.
Color palette: Dipanda royal blue #004cd3 and ice blue #a8d0f3, white snow, naturally dark penguin. Upper sky is a smooth saturated royal blue, becoming lighter toward the horizon. Moderate contrast in the lower scenery; enough deep blue behind the upper text for readable white type.
Constraints: only landscape, snow path and penguin. No giant app tiles, no floating panels, no logos, no Adobe apps, no Ps, Pr, or Ae. No letters, labels, heading, button, branding, watermarks, frames, boxes, currency or dollar symbol in the raster. The website supplies all actual text, buttons and animated arrows as accessible HTML. Do not reproduce the reference's “HAPPINESS”, “Graphic Designer”, watermark, or red outline.
